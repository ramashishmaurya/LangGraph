**Day's Plan: Paneer Delights**

**Breakfast**

* **Paneer Paratha**: A popular North Indian flatbread stuffed with paneer, spices, and herbs.
	+ Ingredients: 1 cup paneer, 1 cup all-purpose flour, 1/4 cup ghee, 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1/2 teaspoon coriander powder, Salt to taste.
	+ Instructions: Mix all the ingredients together, knead the dough, and roll out the paratha. Cook on a tava or griddle.

**Mid-Morning Snack**

* **Paneer Tikka**: Marinated paneer cubes grilled to perfection.
	+ Ingredients: 1 cup paneer, 1/4 cup plain yogurt, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 1 teaspoon garam masala powder, 1 teaspoon cumin powder, 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional), Salt to taste.
	+ Instructions: Marinate the paneer cubes in the yogurt mixture and grill until cooked through.

**Lunch**

* **Palak Paneer**: A creamy spinach curry with paneer.
	+ Ingredients: 1 cup paneer, 2 cups spinach puree, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, Salt to taste.
	+ Instructions: Blend the spinach puree, onion, garlic, and spices together. Add the paneer cubes and simmer until the sauce thickens.

**Evening Snack**

* **Paneer Frankie**: A street-style snack with paneer, spices, and herbs wrapped in a flatbread.
	+ Ingredients: 1 cup paneer, 1 cup all-purpose flour, 1/4 cup ghee, 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1/2 teaspoon coriander powder, Salt to taste.
	+ Instructions: Mix all the ingredients together, knead the dough, and roll out the flatbread. Fill with paneer, spices, and herbs and wrap.

**Dinner**

* **Paneer Jalfrezi**: A spicy stir-fry with paneer, bell peppers, and onions.
	+ Ingredients: 1 cup paneer, 1 onion, 2 bell peppers, 2 cloves garlic, 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, Salt to taste.
	+ Instructions: Stir-fry the onion, bell peppers, and garlic together. Add the paneer cubes and cook until the sauce thickens.

Bhai, this meal plan is a delightful combination of paneer dishes that will satisfy your taste buds and provide you with the necessary nutrients for a healthy day. Enjoy!
